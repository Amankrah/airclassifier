# Separation Efficiency Analysis

Evaluate and optimize your air classification performance with detailed efficiency metrics.

## Key Metrics

### Grade Efficiency

Grade efficiency G(d) is the fraction of particles of size d reporting to the fines:

```python
G(d) = (mass to fines at size d) / (feed mass at size d)
```

The grade efficiency curve characterizes separation:

```
G(d) %
100│                     ╭────────
   │                   ╱
 75│                 ╱
   │               ╱
 50│ - - - - - - ╳ - - - - d_cut
   │           ╱
 25│         ╱
   │       ╱
  0│─────╯
   └─────────────────────────────
    5   10   20   50   100  200 μm
```

### Key Points on the Curve

| Point | Value | Significance |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| d_cut (d₅₀) | 50% grade efficiency | Cut size |
| d₂₅ | 25% grade efficiency | Coarse bypass |
| d₇₅ | 75% grade efficiency | Fines bypass |
| Sharpness | d₇₅/d₂₅ | Separation quality |

### Sharpness Index

Ideal separation: κ = 1 (step function)
Real classifiers: κ = 0.4-0.8

| Sharpness (κ) | Quality | Application |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| > 0.7 | Excellent | Precision fractionation |
| 0.5-0.7 | Good | Standard production |
| 0.3-0.5 | Fair | Rough separation |
| < 0.3 | Poor | Investigate issues |

## Protein Analysis

### Composition Tracking

The simulation tracks protein distribution:

```
Feed (22% protein)
├── Fines (52% protein)  ← Protein-enriched
├── Medium (18% protein)
└── Coarse (8% protein)  ← Starch-enriched
```

### Protein Recovery

```python
Recovery = (mass_fines × protein_fines) / (mass_feed × protein_feed) × 100%
```

Typical performance:

| Strategy | Protein % | Yield % | Recovery % |
|----------|-----------|---------|------------|
| High yield | 45-50% | 35-40% | 75-85% |
| Balanced | 50-55% | 28-33% | 65-75% |
| High purity | 55-65% | 15-22% | 45-60% |

### Purity-Recovery Trade-off

```
Protein %
70│      High Purity
  │          ●
60│        ●
  │      ●       ← Pareto front
50│    ●
  │  ●
40│●
  └────────────────────
   20  40  60  80  100%
        Recovery
```

<Callout type="info">
Higher purity always reduces recovery. The Pareto front shows the best achievable combinations for a given feed and classifier.
</Callout>

## Optimization Tools

### Sensitivity Analysis

Identify critical parameters:

| Parameter | Effect on d_cut | Effect on Sharpness |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Wheel RPM | Strong (-) | Moderate |
| Air Flow | Strong (+) | Weak |
| Feed Rate | Moderate (+) | Moderate (-) |
| Humidity | Weak | Moderate (-) |

### Parameter Effects

```yaml
To decrease d_cut (finer separation):
  - Increase wheel RPM
  - Decrease air flow
  - Decrease feed rate

To increase sharpness:
  - Optimize wheel RPM for feed PSD
  - Reduce feed moisture
  - Ensure uniform feed distribution
```

### Optimization Wizard

Use the built-in optimizer:

1. **Set target**: e.g., "Protein > 50%, Yield > 30%"
2. **Define bounds**: RPM 3000-5000, Air flow 300-500
3. **Run optimization**: Automated search
4. **Review results**: Pareto front visualization

## Diagnostic Charts

### Bypass Analysis

Identify misplaced particles:

**Fines bypass** (coarse particles in fines):
- High feed rate
- Too much air flow
- Turbulence carryover

**Coarse bypass** (fine particles in coarse):
- Low air velocity
- Particle agglomeration
- Electrostatic effects

## Troubleshooting Poor Efficiency

### Low Protein Concentration

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| < 45% protein | Cut too coarse | Increase RPM |
| Broad PSD in fines | Poor sharpness | Check geometry |
| Variable results | Feed inconsistency | Stabilize feed |

### Low Yield

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| < 25% yield | Cut too fine | Decrease RPM |
| High reject | Agglomeration | Reduce humidity |
| Losses | Cyclone inefficiency | Check cyclone |

### Poor Sharpness

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| κ < 0.4 | Turbulence | Reduce air velocity |
| Tails on curve | Bypassing | Check feed distribution |
| Inconsistent | Feed variation | Homogenize feed |
