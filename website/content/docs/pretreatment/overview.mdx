# GP-15 RF Dielectric Heating

The GP-15 module simulates Radio Frequency (RF) dielectric heating for pretreatment of legume seeds before milling. This technology is used to reduce moisture content uniformly while improving the efficiency of downstream processing.

## What is RF Dielectric Heating?

RF dielectric heating uses high-frequency electromagnetic fields (typically 27.12 MHz) to heat materials volumetrically. Unlike conventional heating that works from the outside in, RF heating generates heat throughout the material simultaneously.

### Key Advantages

| Advantage | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Uniform heating** | Heat generated throughout the volume |
| **Selective heating** | Wetter areas heat faster (moisture leveling) |
| **Energy efficient** | Direct energy transfer to material |
| **Rapid processing** | Shorter treatment times than convective drying |
| **Quality preservation** | Lower surface temperatures |

### How It Works

1. **Electric field application**: Material passes between electrodes
2. **Molecular excitation**: Water molecules oscillate with the field
3. **Friction heating**: Molecular motion generates heat
4. **Moisture migration**: Water moves to surface and evaporates

## The GP-15 Machine

The GP-15 is a conveyor-based RF heating system designed for continuous processing of bulk materials.

### Machine Specifications

```yaml
GP-15 Standard Configuration:
  RF Frequency: 27.12 MHz (ISM band)
  Power Output: 15 kW (adjustable)
  Belt Width: 0.9 m
  Belt Length: 3.0 m
  Electrode Gap: 0.05 - 0.15 m (adjustable)
  Belt Speed: 0.01 - 0.10 m/s
  Throughput: 50 - 500 kg/hr
```

### Process Flow

```
[Feed Hopper] → [Spreading System] → [RF Zone] → [Cooling Zone] → [Outlet]
                      ↓                  ↓              ↓
                 Bed formed      Heat + Dry      Stabilize
```

## Simulation Capabilities

The ProteinProcessIO GP-15 simulator models:

### Physics Models

- **Dielectric heating**: Temperature rise from RF energy absorption
- **Heat conduction**: Thermal diffusion within and between seeds
- **Mass transfer**: Moisture migration and surface evaporation
- **Bed dynamics**: Non-uniform bed depth effects

### Material Properties

The simulation includes databases for:

- Yellow peas
- Green peas
- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Faba beans
- Custom materials (user-defined)

Each material has temperature and moisture-dependent properties:
- Dielectric constant (ε')
- Loss factor (ε'')
- Thermal conductivity
- Specific heat capacity
- Density

### Output Parameters

| Output | Unit | Description |

## Typical Applications

### Moisture Reduction

The primary application is reducing seed moisture from storage levels (12-14%) to optimal milling moisture (8-10%).

```yaml
Example Configuration:
  Initial Moisture: 14%
  Target Moisture: 10%
  Initial Temperature: 25°C
  Max Temperature: 65°C
  Belt Speed: 0.05 m/s
  Result: ~180s treatment time
```

### Insect Disinfestation

RF heating can also be used to eliminate insects through thermal treatment:

```yaml
Disinfestation Protocol:
  Target Temperature: >55°C for >1 min
  Typical Setup:
    Belt Speed: 0.03 m/s
    Power: Maximum
    Result: >99% insect mortality
```

<Callout type="warning">
Disinfestation requires careful temperature control. Exceeding 70°C can damage protein functionality.
</Callout>

### Enzyme Inactivation

For reducing antinutritional factors:

```yaml
Enzyme Inactivation:
  Target Temperature: 70-80°C
  Hold Time: 2-5 minutes
  Note: May affect protein solubility
```

## Key Considerations

### Bed Depth Uniformity

Non-uniform bed depth causes uneven heating. The simulation accounts for this by:
- Modeling bed depth variations
- Showing temperature non-uniformity
- Suggesting optimal bed depths

<Callout type="info">
For best results, maintain bed depth below 0.08 m and ensure uniform spreading.
</Callout>

### Runaway Heating

If temperature rises too quickly, thermal runaway can occur. The simulator includes:
- Temperature alarms
- Automatic power reduction modeling
- Safety threshold warnings

### Arc Detection

At high power levels, electrical arcing can occur. The simulation models:
- Voltage gradients
- Arc probability estimation
- Safe operating envelopes
